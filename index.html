INTERSTELLAR RELATIVISTIC FLIGHT MECHANICS

1. Lorentz Factor (γ) & Time Dilation

Special relativity dictates that clocks moving relative to Earth tick slower by the factor γ:

γ = 1 / √(1 - (v/c)²)   |   τ = t_Earth / γ
At 0.20c: γ = 1.02062. Ship time is dilated by 2.02%.

2. Relativistic Kinetic Energy Demand

Kinetic energy accounts for mass gain at relativistic velocities:

E_k = (γ - 1) · m_total · c²
Total energy required to accelerate the craft is calculated dynamically based on payload mass and speed.

3. Relativistic Rocket Equation (Tsiolkovsky)

For onboard propelled spacecraft (Fusion & Antimatter):

v/c = tanh( (v_e / c) · ln(m_initial / m_final) )

Explains why chemical propellants fail exponentially for interstellar distances, while beamed lightsails sidestep the rocket equation entirely by leaving the power source at Earth.
